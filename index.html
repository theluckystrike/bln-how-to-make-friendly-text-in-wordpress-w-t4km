Think about the last time you landed on a blog post and bounced within five seconds. I bet the writing felt cold or overly formal. Maybe it used phrases like "it is imperative to consider" or "one must ensure that." That kind of language puts up a wall between you and your reader.

Friendly text does the opposite. It invites people in. It makes them feel like you're sitting across the table, talking to them over coffee. And here's the kicker. According to a 2022 study by the Nielsen Norman Group, users spend 80% of their time above the fold on web pages, and they read in an F-shaped pattern. How to set up your friendly text workflow in WordPress

You don't need to be a tech wizard to make this work. Here's the simple setup process I follow.

1. Install the BeLikeNative browser extension. It's available for Chrome and works in any text field, including the WordPress block editor. Once it's added, you'll see a small icon in your toolbar.

2. Write your first draft however you want. Don't edit while you write. Just get the ideas down. Let it be rough. Let it be formal. The point is to capture your thoughts without judgment.

3. Select the text you want to make friendlier. Highlight a sentence or a whole paragraph. Then press the keyboard shortcut. In my case, I use Ctrl+Shift+F, but you can customize it to whatever feels natural.

4. Review the rewritten version. BeLikeNative will replace your selected text with a friendlier version. Read it over. Make any small tweaks if needed. Sometimes I change one word or rephrase a clause to match my exact voice.

5. Repeat for any other stiff sections. Usually I only need to run the shortcut on a few parts of each post. The rest of my writing is already pretty conversational. But for those clunky paragraphs, it's a lifesaver.

That's it. The whole process takes maybe five minutes per post. Compare that to the 30 minutes I used to spend manually rewriting, and it's a no brainer.

One last tip for keeping your text friendly

Here's something I've learned after years of blogging. Friendly text isn't just about rewriting sentences. It's about mindset. When you sit down to write, imagine you're emailing a friend who's curious about your topic. Don't try to impress anyone. Just explain things clearly and casually.

Use contractions like "you'll" and "we're." Ask questions. Share personal stories. Keep your paragraphs short, like two to four sentences each. And don't be afraid to leave a little imperfection in your writing. That's what makes it feel human.
